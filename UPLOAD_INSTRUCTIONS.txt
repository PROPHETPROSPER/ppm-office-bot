PPM OFFICE BOT v6 — LIVE ASSIGNMENTS

Do not upload the ZIP itself to GitHub. Extract it first.
Open: https://github.com/prophetprosper/ppm-office-bot/tree/main/app
Click Add file > Upload files. From the extracted ZIP's app folder drag these 4 files:
  office_group.py
  office_tracker.py
  roles.py
  telegram.py
Commit changes to branch main.
Do not upload to a different folder or branch. Never change the Telegram token or Railway variables.

On deployment, commands in linked OFFICE group:
 /setupday@PPMOfficeBot           Owner/coordinator creates unassigned tasks for today
 /pending@PPMOfficeBot            Real open assignment list and statuses
 /assign@PPMOfficeBot Zoom host | open | today | 6 PM
 /mytasks@PPMOfficeBot            Staff sees their own tasks
 /requestaccess@PPMOfficeBot      Staff asks for access; membership alone gives none
 /confirm@PPMOfficeBot ID         Assigned worker confirms their own task
 /decline@PPMOfficeBot ID         Assigned worker declines
 /done@PPMOfficeBot ID            Confirmed worker reports completion

Owner private bot chat:
 /requests                        See who asked for office access
 /staffadd ID coordinator         Grant Michelle coordination (only after identity check)
 /staffadd ID worker              Grant regular worker
 /staffremove ID                  Revoke access

SAFETY: Public live sending and background scheduler remain disabled. No task is assumed confirmed.
