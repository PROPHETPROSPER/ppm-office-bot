# Teaching and membership design

## Free private mentorship — approx. 80 members
**Promise:** steady biblical growth and relationships; never imply that free members cannot receive prayer or blessings.

- Four-week rolling cycle for newcomers (join anytime): Knowing Scripture; Prayer & Consecration; Discernment & Wisdom; Serving Faithfully.
- Two useful posts weekly: one Scripture reflection + one applied discussion prompt; one public teaching link where relevant.
- New member bot welcome with Start Here resources and group rules.
- Monthly open Q&A or group-led discussion, capacity permitting.
- Referral pathway to Spiritual Foundation is opt-in, not an obligation.

## Paid Spiritual Foundation — approx. 35 members
**Promise:** planned curriculum, organized lessons, structured accountability, feedback and direct learning support.

Saturday **2 PM ET** class, normally 75 minutes. On October 24, use an assignment because Watchman Series is planned for 2–4 PM.

**Twelve-week curriculum**
1. Scripture reading methods and context
2. Salvation, identity and spiritual maturity
3. Habits of prayer, worship and Scripture
4. Biblical models of prayer and intercession
5. Fasting, spiritual discipline and well-being
6. Building a sustainable prayer routine
7. Testing impressions with Scripture
8. Discernment and the responsible use of gifts
9. Wisdom, accountability and spiritual ethics
10. Service as leadership
11. Character, boundaries and pastoral responsibility
12. Capstone: a supervised application project and individualized feedback

Each member receives a lesson note, 1 assignment and 1 scheduled question/feedback window. Do not promise private prophecy or guaranteed spiritual results as a paid benefit.

## Teaching to content pipeline
Six Monday–Saturday YouTube teachings weekly, with Sunday off.
Per teaching: exactly **three carousel slides**, one strong short reel, a second reel only if the material supports it. AI only drafts from verified transcripts. Designer handles visual production; Amoy/Alex potential video editing; Michelle/approved reviewer authorizes publication.
